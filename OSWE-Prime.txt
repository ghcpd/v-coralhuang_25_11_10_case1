Notes on design choices and why they fix the issue
- Why session-level event listeners:
  - Binding events to a session class guarantees the handlers run for every model contained in that session, regardless of model.
- Why isinstance(obj, SearchableMixin):
  - The handlers filter objects that need indexing by their type rather than always assuming a particular model; that prevents misses when new models adopt the mixin.
- Registering listeners via a centralized API:
  - SearchableMixin.register_listeners(db) consolidates and centralizes registration and avoids forgetting to bind event listeners for new models.

Edge cases that are handled
- No double-registration:
  - The session class gets a _searchable_listeners_registered attribute so register_listeners is idempotent.
- Rollbacks:
  - _after_rollback clears pending changes; prevents incorrect indexing on failed transactions.
- Non-searchable models:
  - Objects not inheriting the mixin are ignored silently.
